How this works

There is no sign-up and no password. Everything — the lessons, the marking, your progress — runs inside your own browser. Your code is never uploaded, so nobody can see your work but you.

Because progress is stored on the device you are using, it will not follow you from your phone to a lab computer. If you clear your browser data it resets. That is the trade for a site that needs no account and costs nothing to run.
